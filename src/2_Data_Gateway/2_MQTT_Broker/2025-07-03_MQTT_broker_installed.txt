2025-07-04 QMTT broker, mosquitto app installed on the Raspberry Pi CM5
to have this CM5 with the mosquitto app running, serving as a QMTT broker
for several microcontoller devices here in the house, like:
1) an Arduino Uno R4 (Renesas RA4M1 (Arm® Cortex®-M4) + ESP32-S3 for Wi-Fi®/Bluetooth® connectivity) WiFi in the role of MQTT Publisher
2) a Pimoroni Presto (RP2350) in the role of MQTT Subscriber
3) (future:) a Unexpected Maker SQUiXL (ESP32S3)

I installed the mosquitto app to this RPiCM5 to have not the need to use the big desktop PC Paul5 (and run all night, with the noise of its ventilators and the heat this desktop PC emits), The box in which the RPiCM5 is mounted stays fairly cool.

IP address of the RPiCM5 is: 192.168._.___
